# MAGNUM — Wire compositions

## A. Match night

Full-screen human hero → overlapping community spread → type-only manifesto → sticky five-stage evening → horizontal formats → dense gallery → poster CTA.

**Strength:** the whole site reads as one evening unfolding in real time. **Risk:** needs disciplined transitions so it does not become a festival template.

## B. Club journal

Editorial cover → long single-column story → image/caption folios → portraits → interviews → practical booking appendix.

**Strength:** mature and photographic. **Risk:** too static for a sport/community product and weakens the interaction evidence.

## C. Tournament programme

Scoreboard hero → schedule grid → player stories → rulebook typography → results/media wall → next game CTA.

**Strength:** unmistakably sport. **Risk:** suggests a formal tournament and results system that the source materials do not verify.

## Selected: A / Match night

It matches the confirmed content and the original recordings: people arrive, learn the game, sit at the table, pause, and finish the evening together. The signature interaction is the active evening stage controlling the main photograph and progress line. The three intended wow moments are the cinematic entrance, the interactive evening sequence, and the immersive real-photo gallery.
