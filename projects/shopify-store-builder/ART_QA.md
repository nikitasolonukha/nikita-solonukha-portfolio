# Lumina Form V3 — manual art QA

## Direction

Object-led high-end ecommerce. The lamp is the visual system: matte black form, white light field and a restrained chartreuse purchase moment. The builder remains a separate functional product surface.

## Three strong moments

1. Hero type intersects the real lamp photograph without splitting the viewport.
2. Object Anatomy changes image and copy through one intentional interaction.
3. The purchase scene combines proposition, product and action in one poster-like composition.
