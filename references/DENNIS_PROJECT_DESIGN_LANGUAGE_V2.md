# Dennis Snellenberg — project design language V2

Visual review of the eleven case pages on 22 September 2026. These are observations of the portfolio case pages and their visible hero media, not a claim that every linked client site was audited. All eleven share a deliberately restrained case shell: generous white space, plain large project title, three small credit columns, then project-specific media. The lesson is to keep a stable editorial frame while allowing the actual product imagery to carry the identity. Copying this shell into the product UI would miss the point.

| Case | Visual language visible in case | Useful lesson for Nikita's products |
|---|---|---|
| [TWICE](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/twice) | Red-clad athlete against a pale field; action and scale, rather than decorative UI. | Let one real action dominate a frame. |
| [The Damai](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/the-damai) | Architecture, foliage and warm material appear immediately after a very quiet introduction. | Use material and location for atmosphere when the project is physical. |
| [FABRIC](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/fabric) | Saturated red sculptural form cropped against black; contrast comes from the object. | Make the product image and its silhouette the visual system. |
| [Aanstekelijk](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/aanstekelijk) | Purple/blue-lit retail photography supplies the energy. | Photographic colour can replace generic gradient decoration. |
| [Base Create](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/base-create) | Dark textured product/mobile imagery creates depth within the neutral case frame. | Show texture and real screens at useful scale. |
| [AVVR](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/avvr) | Human interaction photography warms an otherwise quiet, minimal presentation. | Give service/people projects a human point of view. |
| [GraphicHunters](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/graphichunters) | Close crop of a physical shoe on deep green; shape and surface read before copy. | Use extreme product detail only where the item supports it. |
| [Future Goals](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/future-goals) | Sunlit outdoor portrait adds motion and lived context. | Explain a goal-led product with a believable user situation. |
| [Atypikal](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/atypikal) | Sparse white marks on black; identity makes deliberate use of near-empty space. | Typography and restraint can create character without UI chrome. |
| [One:Nil](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/one-nil) | Cinematic portrait, dark blue lighting and direct gaze. | Anchor a narrative in a strong human image. |
| [Andy Hardy](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work/andy-hardy) | Documentary camera portrait and muted natural tones. | Use honest media for creative work; avoid synthetic stock atmosphere. |

## Transfer rule

For each product demo: retain its real mechanism and user flow; choose one visual idea from its own content; give that idea a clear composition, type scale and controlled motion. Assess screenshots at 1440, 768 and 390. A case frame can support the product but cannot compensate for unfinished product screens. Further client-site and lower-section checks are still required before treating this reference audit as comprehensive.
